The importance of the instructor
Why will a student want to learn something hard?
1. Love the teacher (their passion for knowledge)
2. Has an interest in the course content
3. Has a need that the course can (partially) fulfill
4. Has a plan/dream
💡 The instructor is the key — they can influence all four fundamental motivations.

Reinforcement-learning-style learning
• State: reasoning, effort, and response.
• Trace: attempts, wrong steps, rereading,
questions, and feedback responses.
• Next action: material, exercise, depth, and
timing based on current evidence.
• Reward: based on the trace, next action, and
state; combine progress, AI feedback, and
curiosity.
💡 The current learning system is inefficient; choose a locally optimal policy to improve learning efficiency
significantly.

Conclusions
AI changes both how students learn and what they need to learn.
Scientific research into AI + education
• Exploit AI’s capabilities, while keeping the learner’s reasoning visible.
• Understand and model how people learn, struggle, and transfer.
• Use technical and scientific methods: build, compare, measure, falsify.
• Develop and iterate quickly as the models change.
