Why the obvious fixes do not settle the problem
First idea What remains unresolved
Solve with more precision
This error came from measurement noise. Exact arithmetic still amplifies
it.
Use the pseudoinverse
For this invertible matrix it equals the inverse. Small positive singular
values still give large gains.
Drop the difference entirely
Both voices share the sum. Discarding their difference also discards real
separation information.
Always use stronger shrinkage
Shrinkage reduces coefficients toward zero. A larger penalty reduces
noise amplification but can also suppress true signal, so expected error
can rise.

Three limits to keep in view
1. Missing information stays missing. A null direction is a coefficient change that produces no
change in the measurements. The squared-norm penalty chooses zero for a coefficient the data do
not determine; it does not recover that coefficient.
2. Noise in a new response remains. A penalty cannot remove response variance left even when the
conditional mean is known.
3. The best penalty is still unknown. Training residuals do not select the best predictive tradeoff.
Later selection needs independent evidence or another justified criterion.
As 𝜆 → 0+
, finite-dimensional ridge approaches the least-squares solution with the smallest Euclidean
norm. It equals the usual OLS inverse formula only under full column rank.
